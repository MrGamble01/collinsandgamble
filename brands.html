Before you brief us

What makes a fit.

We're selective on the brand side too — because the format only works for products that earn an honest yes. If three of the four below sound like your product, let's talk.

01

A product that demos well on camera

The on-site review format rewards things you can show, hold, unbox, or use on screen. If a 30-second video can make the value obvious, it's a fit.

02

A listing worth the lift

A live Amazon PDP with decent images and copy already in place. We lift conversion on the listing — we don't rebuild a broken one from scratch.

03

Willing to hear the honest take

We don't pre-approve scripts. The whole point of an on-site review is that it's real. Brands that need a word-for-word script aren't a fit for the format or for us.

04

Ready to read conversion, not reach

We report clicks, conversions, and lift on the ASIN — not vanity impressions. If PDP performance is the number you care about, we're aligned.

How it works

From brief to lift.

One brief in, a vetted creator set out, on-site review videos on the ASINs that matter, and a report that ties back to PDP performance — not vanity reach.

  1. 01

    You send the brief

    The form above, or a call. We look at the product, the listing, and the goal — and tell you honestly whether the on-site format will move it.

    Day 0
  2. 02

    We scope the fit & match creators

    If it's a fit, we pull a vetted creator set from the roster — matched to your category and audience — and scope the campaign with you.

    Day 1 – 5
  3. 03

    Products ship, videos get filmed

    We coordinate the sends and the briefs end to end. Creators film honest on-site reviews. No spec buys, no chasing, no script approval cycles.

    Weeks 1 – 3
  4. 04

    Videos go live — and we report by ASIN

    Review videos land on your product detail pages. You get reporting tied to clicks, conversions, and lift on the listing itself. Repeat as your catalog grows.

    Week 4+
