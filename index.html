Both workflows

Two paths. One engine.

Creators on one side. Brands on the other. Same back office running both — here's the version each side actually lives in.

For creators

Open the box. Press record. Get paid.

  1. 01
    Apply

    Send us your Amazon storefront and a short intro. We respond within a few business days.

  2. 02
    Open the box

    Products ship to your door. Briefs ride along. Nothing to buy, nothing to chase.

  3. 03
    Press record

    Film an honest on-site Amazon review. Upload. We handle everything before, after, and around it.

  4. 04
    Get paid

    Amazon commissions and brand fees compound. We report on what's working and line up the next deal.

For brands

Brief us. We ship. Conversion lifts.

  1. 01
    Brief us

    Tell us about the product, the ASINs, and what you're trying to move. We respond within a few business days.

  2. 02
    We match

    We pair your product with vetted Amazon Influencers on our roster and run the brief end-to-end.

  3. 03
    Reviews go live

    On-site review videos hit your Amazon listings. Honest, on-platform, where shoppers actually decide.

  4. 04
    Conversion lifts

    We report back by ASIN — clicks, conversion, lift. You scale what worked. We line up the next campaign.
