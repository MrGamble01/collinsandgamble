Privacy

The short,
honest version.

We collect the minimum we need to respond to you and run a small business website. We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers, or pass it to anyone outside the agency. Below is everything we actually do.

What we collect

From the contact and apply forms: your name, email address, and whatever you choose to put in the message and supporting fields (storefront URL, social handle, audience size, etc.). On the apply form, the categories you cover and the optional earnings range. That's it — we don't collect anything you didn't type into the form yourself.

From analytics: we use Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights, which record page views, referrers, device type, country, and basic performance numbers. It's cookieless — no individuals are tracked, no profile is built, no data is sent to advertisers. The summary is anonymous and aggregated.

Fonts and CDN: we load fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google sees your IP address when you visit the site. Standard for the open web, but worth saying out loud.

What we do with it

Form submissions arrive in our inbox via Resend. We read them, decide whether to respond, and respond. If it's a roster application, we use the details to evaluate fit — that's the whole point. We may keep your note in our inbox for reference in case we want to come back to it later.

We use analytics to understand which pages people read, which ones get skipped, and whether the site is fast enough. It's how we decide what to improve. No individual user is ever identified.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data. Not to anyone, for any reason.
  • We don't share it with advertisers, brokers, or third-party marketing tools.
  • We don't run ads, so there are no ad pixels, retargeting tags, or behavioral profiles.
  • We don't send unsolicited email. If you didn't write to us, we won't write to you.

Who else sees it

Three companies process data on our behalf:

  • Resend delivers form submissions to our inbox.
  • Vercel hosts the site and runs the analytics.
  • Calendly handles bookings if you use the "book a call" link — they collect what you'd expect for scheduling (name, email, time).

That's the full list. We don't pass your data to anyone else.

Your data, your call

You can ask us to delete your details at any time, for any reason — no explanation required. Email hello@collinsandgamble.com and we'll remove what we have within a few business days. If you're in the EU/UK, you have the additional rights granted by GDPR: access, correction, portability, restriction, objection. Same email reaches us for any of those.

Updates to this notice

If the way we handle data ever changes meaningfully — new tools, new categories of data, anything that affects you — we'll update this page and date the change below. We won't quietly start doing something different.
