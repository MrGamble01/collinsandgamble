How it works

Creators film. Creators get paid.
We take care of everything else.

One engine, two sides. Creators on one side, brands on the other. Both sides plug into the same back office — sourcing, deals, storefront, content, and analytics — run by us, end to end.

Side 01

For creators

You came for the Amazon Influencer Program. You stayed because filming reviews is the only part you actually like. We take care of everything else.

What we run for you

  • Brand & product sourcing — boxes at your door, already briefed
  • Deal & contract negotiation — rates, exclusivity, deliverables
  • Amazon storefront build-out — categories, idea lists, photos, copy
  • Content coaching — hooks, lengths, formats that convert on Amazon
  • Performance analytics — what earned, what didn't, what's next
  • Brand relationships — outreach, briefings, and renewals

What you do

  • Open the box.
  • Film an honest on-site review.
  • Upload to Amazon.
  • Pick up your commissions and fees.

No spreadsheets. No cold emails. No buying products to review on speculation.

Side 02

For brands

Amazon shoppers don't trust your hero image. They trust a 30-second on-site review video from a creator who's actually opened the box. We get your product into those videos.

What we do

  • Match your product to vetted Amazon Influencers
  • Coordinate the send and the brief — end to end
  • Negotiate creator deals and handle paperwork
  • Place on-site review videos on your Amazon listings
  • Report conversion lift back, by ASIN
  • Run repeat campaigns as your catalog expands

What you get

  • On-site review videos on your Amazon PDP
  • Real creator audiences — vetted, not bots
  • Conversion lift on the listing itself
  • Reporting tied directly to your ASINs

We don't pre-approve scripts. The whole point of an on-site review is that it's honest.

FAQ

A few questions, answered plainly.

Don't see yours? Ask us directly.

Which creators do you sign?

Approved Amazon Influencers with a working storefront and a real audience that watches their on-site reviews. We're picky — most applications are declined, usually because the storefront isn't doing the work yet.

Do creators have to buy the products?

Never. Products ship to the creator from the brand or from us, already briefed and cleared. No spec buys, no out-of-pocket expense.

What does it cost a creator?

Nothing upfront. We're paid from the brand side and on a commission of any direct brand deals we negotiate for the creator. If the creator doesn't earn, neither do we.

What does it cost a brand?

Engagements are priced by scope — number of creators, products, and the work we run around the campaign. Most brand engagements start with a single-product test before scaling.

How are creators paid?

Amazon commissions go straight from Amazon to the creator's payout account. Direct brand deals we negotiate are paid through us, with the agency commission deducted before payout.

Do you guarantee a positive review?

No, and we wouldn't want to. On-site Amazon reviews convert because they're honest. We brief; the creator films what they actually think. Brands that need a pre-approved script should not work with us.
