The practice

Three disciplines, one engagement.

Every creator we represent works across the same three disciplines. The proportion shifts with the chapter — strategy heavier early, content steady throughout, partnerships once the brand is sharp.

Discipline 01

Career & Brand Strategy

Positioning, IP, and the plan for the next two years. We help creators decide what their work is actually about — and what it isn't.

What's included

  • Audience read & creator audit
  • Positioning & narrative arc
  • Brand pillars & voice guide
  • Two-year career roadmap
  • IP & creator-owned ideas
  • Quarterly strategy reviews

What it gives you

  • A clear answer to what the work is about — and what it isn't
  • A short list of brands worth saying yes to
  • Calendar pillars that ladder to the longer story
  • A roadmap you can speak to in your own words
Discipline 02

Content & Production

The unglamorous half of the job: calendars, shoot days, edits, and the discipline of posting like you mean it. We build the system, the creator stays the voice.

Engagements

  • Editorial calendars & content pillars
  • Shoot planning — location, crew, cadence
  • Edit support across short-form, long-form, photo
  • Voice, format & series development
  • Platform cadence (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, newsletter)
  • Honest performance reads, not flattering ones

Deliverables

  • A weekly rhythm the creator can keep without resentment
  • Pillars that work across platforms, not against each other
  • A library of formats that survive the year
  • Reviews tied to the strategy, not to vanity

FAQ

A few questions, answered plainly.

Don't see yours? Ask us directly.

Who do you sign?

We represent creators across lifestyle, fashion, food, wellness, travel and design — usually with a real audience already showing up and a point of view worth investing in. Most of our roster comes through introduction.

How do you charge?

Commission, structured per discipline. Strategy and content sit inside the engagement; partnerships are commissioned on the deals we negotiate. No setup fees, no monthly retainer.

Do you take on creators who already have a manager?

Rarely, and only after a clean handover. If you're under contract elsewhere, please don't send work yet — finish that conversation first.

What size audience do you work with?

Audience size matters less than the work, the voice, and the runway. Most of the creators we sign sit somewhere between fifty thousand and a couple of million — but the read is qualitative, not numeric.

What's the minimum term?

We sign for a year, with an honest review at six months. Short engagements don't suit the way we work.
