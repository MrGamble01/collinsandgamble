How it works

Creators film. Creators get paid.
We take care of everything else.

One engine, two sides. Creators on one side, brands on the other. Both sides plug into the same back office — sourcing, deals, storefront, content, and analytics — run by us, end to end.

Side 01

You came for the Amazon Influencer Program. You stayed because filming reviews is the only part you actually like. We take care of everything else.

  • Deal & contract negotiation — rates, exclusivity, deliverables

FAQ

A few questions, answered plainly.

Don't see yours? Ask us directly.

Creators

Which creators do you sign?

We sign approved Amazon Influencers with a working storefront and an audience that actually watches their on-site reviews. We're picky. Most applications are declined, usually because the storefront isn't doing the work yet.

Do creators have to buy the products?

Never. Products ship from the brand or from us, already briefed. You never pay out of pocket.

What does it cost a creator?

Nothing upfront. We're paid by the brand side and through a commission on any direct brand deals we negotiate for you. If you don't earn, neither do we.

How are creators paid?

Amazon commissions go straight from Amazon to your payout account. We never touch them. Direct brand deals we negotiate are paid through us, and our agency commission is taken out before payout.

Do I keep my storefront and my content?

Yes. Your storefront, your Amazon account, and your videos are yours. We work inside your setup with the access you grant, and you can revoke that access at any time.

I'm not approved for the Amazon Influencer Program yet. Can you help?

We don't manage creators before they're approved. Our journal post on getting approved walks through what reviewers look for. Apply once you're in.

How many products will I get?

That depends on your category, your conversion history, and the brands in rotation that month. We'd rather send you fewer products that fit than flood your porch.

Can I turn down a product?

Yes, always. If a product isn't a fit for you or your audience, say no. We'll find another.

What if I don't like the product?

Say so on camera. An honest review is the job. If a product has a safety issue, tell us first so we can flag it to the brand.

Is there a contract? How long?

Yes, a written management agreement with a plain exit clause. Terms are set per creator. Ask us for a sample before you sign anything.

Do I have to disclose that a product was free?

Yes. Follow the FTC's endorsement guides and Amazon's program policies every time. We brief you on both, and we won't work with creators who skip disclosure.

Brands

What does it cost a brand?

Pricing depends on scope: how many creators, how many products, and the work we run around the campaign. Most brands start with a single-product test.

Do you guarantee positive reviews?

No, and we wouldn't want to. On-site reviews convert because they're honest. We write the brief, and the creator films what they actually think. If you need a pre-approved script, we're not the right partner.

Can we see the video before it goes live?

You'll see the brief, and you can correct factual errors such as a wrong spec, a wrong size, or a wrong claim. You can't edit the opinion.

How do you report results?

By ASIN. We track which videos are live on your listings and show listing performance over the campaign window. You'll get a plain summary, not a 40-tab dashboard.

Which categories do you work in?

Most consumer categories sold on Amazon. We'll tell you on the first call whether our roster fits your product. If it doesn't, we'll say so.

Who will know we worked together?

Only the creators involved, plus whatever disclosure the law requires on the content itself. We don't publish client lists or logos without written permission.

A person reads every note. Expect a reply within two business days.
