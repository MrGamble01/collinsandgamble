About the agency

Creators film. Creators get paid.
We take care of everything else.

That's the whole pitch. Underneath it is a back office most creators on the Amazon Influencer Program don't have — brand sourcing, product sends, contracts, storefront, content, analytics. We run all of it, so the creator never has to leave the camera to chase a deal or rebuild a listing.

What we believe

Six principles that decide how we work.

We don't sell a methodology. We have working habits — earned in real campaigns — that we apply to every engagement, both sides of the table.

i

The creator's time belongs on camera.

Every minute a creator spends sourcing products or chasing brand contacts is a minute they're not earning. We take that work off them, completely.

ii

Honest reviews convert better.

Amazon shoppers can tell when a video is performed. We brief; we don't write the script. The creator films what they actually think — and the numbers reward it.

iii

The storefront is the store.

A creator's Amazon storefront is doing more work than their Instagram grid ever will. We treat it like the revenue page it is.

iv

Two sides, one standard.

Brands and creators get the same operating practice. The bar doesn't change based on who's paying which invoice.

v

Numbers, not vibes.

Every engagement reports back to actual Amazon performance — clicks, conversions, commissions. Reach is a leading indicator; PDP conversion is the verdict.

vi

No surprises, ever.

Plain contracts, plain reports, plain math. The creator knows what they earn. The brand knows what they bought. We don't get cute with either.

How we're set up

One engine, run by people who answer their email.

We keep the creator roster intentionally small and the brand roster intentionally curated. Every engagement is run by someone who actually knows the work — not a junior between you and the system.
