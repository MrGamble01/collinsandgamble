About the agency

We represent careers, not campaigns.

Collins & Gamble was founded on a small idea — that the creators worth backing are the ones building something on a longer clock than the algorithm rewards. Our job is to give them the room, the rigor, and the partnerships to keep building.

What we believe

Six principles that decide how we work.

We don't sell a methodology. We have working habits — argued for, refined, and applied to every creator on the roster.

Roster over revenue.

A small roster is the whole job. The moment we stop knowing the work in detail, we stop being useful to the people we represent.

Strategy upstream of content.

We don't open the calendar until we agree on what the creator stands for. Posts without a position are noise — and noise doesn't compound.

Editorial over campaign.

Careers are made between launches. We protect the in-between work as carefully as the headline moments.

Deals chosen, not chased.

Most inbound is politely declined. The best partnerships look slow from the outside and obvious in hindsight.

Measured in years.

We sign for the arc of a career. Quarterly reviews are honest, not optimistic; we don't grade ourselves on weeks.

Honest with the creator.

Plain reports, direct conversations, contracts you can read in five minutes. No theater, no managing-up.

How we're set up

An agency sized for attention.

We keep the roster intentionally small. Every creator we represent works directly with a partner of the agency — there is no junior layer between the work and the people responsible for it.

By introduction

A selective roster

We accept a small number of creators each year, almost always through introduction. We say no to far more than we sign.

Senior-led

Every creator, a partner

Each creator on the roster works directly with a partner of the agency. There is no junior layer to filter through.

Long-form

Built for years

Engagements are written for the long arc. The first review is quarterly — and so is the hundredth.
