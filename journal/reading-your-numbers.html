Reading your numbers:
four metrics that matter.

Creator dashboards are full of numbers. Most don't change what you do on Monday. These four do.

Category · Reporting Reading time · 5 minutes By · The agency Published · July 2026

Creator dashboards are full of numbers. Most don't change what you do on Monday. These four do.

1. Earnings by video, not by month

A monthly total tells you whether you had a good month. Earnings by video tell you why. Sort your videos by what they earned. The top slice is your playbook.

2. Conversion, not views

A video with fewer views that converts well is worth more than a widely seen one that doesn't. Look at the share of clicks that turn into purchases. That's what tells you which products and formats your audience actually trusts.

3. Earnings by category

Group your earnings by category: kitchen, beauty, tools, home, and so on. Most creators find one or two categories carry their storefront. That's where to say yes to more products, and where to price brand deals higher.

4. Time to first sale

How long after you upload does a video make its first sale? Fast starters tell you about demand. Slow-but-steady videos are your long-tail earners, so keep them current.

What to mostly ignore

  • Likes and comments on an Amazon video. Nice to see, loosely tied to earnings.
  • One-day spikes. Look at 30-day windows.
  • Comparing yourself to other creators' screenshots. Different categories, different audiences.

Turn it into one decision a month

Every month, answer three questions:

  1. What earned most, and can I film more like it?
  2. What category should I say yes to more often?
  3. Which old videos need a refresh?

That's the monthly report we send creators on our roster: plain numbers and one or two changes. Apply here.
