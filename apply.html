Before you apply

What we look for.

We're picky on purpose. The roster stays small so the work stays good. If three of the four below sound like you, send it through.

01

Approved on the Amazon Influencer Program

You've been accepted into AIP and have a live storefront URL we can visit. If you're still applying to Amazon, get through that first — we can't accelerate it.

02

A real audience that actually watches

Not the biggest. The realest. We care more about consistent on-site review views and storefront conversion than follower counts on other platforms.

03

Honest on camera

You film what you actually think. You'll work with brands that want the real take, not a pre-approved script. If you need the script, we're not a fit.

04

Ready to be managed

You want to film and get paid, not run a business behind the scenes. You're OK with us handling brand outreach, deals, and storefront work on your behalf.

What happens next

From inbox to roster.

We don't ghost. Even the no responses come with a reason — so you know whether to come back later or not at all.

  1. 01

    You send it

    The form above lands in our application inbox. A person — not a form bot — picks it up.

    Day 0
  2. 02

    We read it & visit your storefront

    We look at the actual work: storefront curation, on-site review pacing, category fit, audience signal. Maybe ten minutes per application — but slow on purpose.

    Day 1 – 3
  3. 03

    You hear back, either way

    Yes goes to a quick intro call. No comes with a reason. Maybe-later means we want to keep talking when something changes — usually a category opening or a storefront milestone.

    Within a week
  4. 04

    If yes: intro & onboarding

    Short call. We walk through the management agreement, current brand pipeline, and what the first 30 days look like. Storefront review starts that week.
